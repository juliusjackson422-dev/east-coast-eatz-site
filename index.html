Philly, parked in Ohio

The roll is the whole thing.

A cheesesteak is four ingredients and nowhere to hide. Get the bread wrong and the rest of it does not matter.

So the rolls come from Amoroso’s in Philadelphia — the same hearth-baked roll the corner shops back east have used for a century. Everything else is chopped and griddled to order on the flat-top while you stand there: thin-sliced beef or diced chicken, onions if you want them, cheese sauce, provolone or American over the top.

That is the entire trick. It has been the entire trick since 2013.

The beef hits the flat-top when you order it. Onions go on beside it if you said onions, cheese goes over the top, and the whole thing goes straight into an Amoroso roll and straight out of the window. That is the only speed it runs at.
